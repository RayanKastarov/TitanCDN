ABOUT US

TITANCDN • WHO WE ARE

We turn any public web page into clean, structured data. You give TitanCDN a link and a plain-English instruction, and it returns exactly the information you asked for.

OUR COMPANY

TitanCDN is an AI-powered web data platform. Send a URL and an instruction such as “get only the company names”, and Titan fetches the page, filters it with AI and returns tidy JSON through one simple API.

We built it for teams that need reliable data without maintaining fragile scrapers: price monitoring, market research, lead lists and catalog tracking.

Plans are measured in characters processed, so you only pay for the volume you actually use. Start with a free trial, then scale from Starter up to Mega Factory.
